# Functional Requirements

`FR-01` The system shall register clients, authenticate users, return JWT bearer tokens, and expose the current user.

`FR-02` The system shall enforce client, agent, and manager permissions at API and route level.

`FR-03` The catalogue shall list active properties with pagination and filters and expose details, features, images, and coordinates.

`FR-04` Clients shall create, view, update, and delete their search requests and receive matching agents and lead scores.

`FR-05` Clients shall add/remove favorites and create/update/delete saved searches that can be executed against current listings.

`FR-06` Agents shall maintain profiles, availability, territories, property-type expertise, and owned listings.

`FR-07` Authorized users shall create conversations, exchange messages, track unread state, and delete permitted records.

`FR-08` Clients shall request appointments; clients, assigned agents, and managers shall access appropriate status/reschedule workflows.

`FR-09` The system shall persist notifications and support list, unread count, mark-read, mark-all-read, and delete operations.

`FR-10` Managers shall administer users, agents, properties, contracts, appointments, conversations, market areas, and knowledge articles.

`FR-11` Managers shall view overview, property, agent, client, appointment, engagement, lead, contract, heat-map, and time-series analytics.

`FR-12` The assistant shall classify supported intents, retrieve relevant active knowledge articles, cite sources, and return suggested actions.

`FR-13` The platform shall expose personalized recommendations, lead-conversion prediction, sale/rent price estimation, and ML model metadata with safe fallback behavior.

`FR-14` Clients shall calculate investment metrics from supplied property and financing assumptions.

`FR-15` Clients shall view their contracts and the property linked to an authorized contract.

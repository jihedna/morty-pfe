# Constraints and Assumptions

## Constraints

The project operates under the following constraints:

- **Limited access to real RE/MAX data**: Access to comprehensive historical RE/MAX data may be restricted due to privacy policies, data protection regulations, or internal policies, potentially limiting the amount of real data available for training and testing.

- **Confidentiality of client information**: Strict confidentiality requirements govern the handling of client personal information, transaction history, and sensitive data, necessitating robust security measures and data anonymization.

- **Incomplete historical data**: Historical data may be incomplete, inconsistent, or missing certain fields, requiring data cleaning, imputation, or synthetic data generation to support system functionality.

- **Inconsistent property records**: Property records may contain inconsistencies in formatting, terminology, or completeness, requiring standardization and validation processes.

- **Limited project duration**: The project must be completed within a defined timeframe, constraining the scope of features that can be implemented and tested thoroughly.

- **API service costs**: Usage of external APIs such as Google Maps, OpenAI, or other services may incur costs that must be managed within budget constraints, potentially limiting usage volumes or requiring cost optimization strategies.

- **AI response accuracy**: The accuracy and reliability of AI-generated responses may vary, requiring careful prompt engineering, testing, and fallback mechanisms to ensure acceptable quality.

- **Legal information requiring regular updates**: Legal procedures, tax rates, and regulatory information change over time, requiring a maintenance process to keep the knowledge base current and accurate.

- **Limited availability of geographic coordinates**: Not all property addresses may have accurate or complete geographic coordinates, potentially affecting map visualization and location-based features.

## Assumptions

The project is based on the following assumptions:

- **RE/MAX Smile may provide selected anonymized data**: The agency may provide access to selected anonymized datasets for development and testing purposes, subject to confidentiality agreements and data protection requirements.

- **Synthetic data may be used when real data is unavailable**: When real data is not available or insufficient, synthetic or mock data may be generated to support development, testing, and demonstration of system features.

- **Agents can update their availability**: Agents will have the ability and willingness to update their availability status, workload information, and expertise profiles through the platform to improve matching accuracy.

- **Property records contain enough information for search and filtering**: Available property records contain sufficient structured information to support the search, filtering, and recommendation features planned for the platform.

- **Approved legal and market documents can be used by the chatbot**: RE/MAX Smile will provide or approve the legal documents, market information, and knowledge base content that the chatbot will use to answer client questions.

- **The first version will be a web application**: The initial implementation will be a web-based application accessible through standard web browsers, with mobile responsiveness but not native mobile applications.

- **The first geographic scope is Grand Tunis**: The initial geographic coverage will be limited to the Grand Tunis region, with property data and agent expertise concentrated in this area.

These constraints and assumptions will be validated and refined throughout the project lifecycle as more information becomes available and as development progresses.

# Project Objectives

The implemented platform is intended to:

1. Centralize users, agents, listings, client requirements, communications, appointments, contracts, and market data.
2. Reduce the time required for clients to discover properties and connect with a relevant agent.
3. Make matching explainable through visible compatibility factors and lead qualification scores.
4. Give agents a single workflow for listings, leads, conversations, appointments, and notifications.
5. Give managers operational controls and measurable agency-wide analytics.
6. Support client decisions with maps, favorites, saved searches, recommendations, investment scoring, and price estimation.
7. Answer common real-estate questions from manager-maintained knowledge-base content with source attribution.
8. Use ML where data and artifacts are available while preserving deterministic fallbacks.
9. Enforce role isolation and ownership checks for sensitive records.
10. Keep schema evolution reproducible through Alembic migrations and validate behavior with backend and frontend tests.

These are product objectives, not claims of production readiness or guaranteed financial, legal, or predictive accuracy.
